# Evidence levels and merge semantics

- Software reproducibility: imports, hashes, tests and deterministic reruns. These do not establish biological validity.
- Retrospective predictive evidence: fits evaluated on already used studies, with task and target-information budgets recorded. Never pooled across units or splits.
- Conditional identification/design: true only under explicit measurement and correspondence assumptions. An optimized coupling is not an observed biological pairing.
- Independent confirmation: a newly locked study/preparation and genuinely unexposed model-selection process. No current branch is labelled as having established the full functional-state claim.
- Mechanistic intervention: a prospective change of a proposed explanatory process with the predicted response. No such finding is claimed.

This integration is conservation plus an explicit active route, not a statistical meta-analysis or a universal algorithm. Histories that added only protocols retain that status. The local consolidated package restores the missing staged source/evidence archives and records their exact SHA256. Large raw datasets and full prediction arrays remain separately indexed.
