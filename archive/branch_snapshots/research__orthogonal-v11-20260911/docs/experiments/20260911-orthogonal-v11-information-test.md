## Verified scope and constraints
Original assay roles are P100 3h, L1000 6h and GCP 24h; doses vary by compound. Match exact publisher base_cell_id, BRD pert_id and exact decimal dose, never dose bins. NPC.TAK maps to NPC only via publisher base_cell_id; flag it, and report a no-NPC sensitivity without claiming genotype/culture identity. Physical preparation linkage across assays is unavailable. Duplicate replicate labels or PRM/DIA remeasurements are not independent cultures. RNA metadata has 1694 rows but 1667 measured rows; 8/16 measured RNA plates have no deposited DMSO. No controls are borrowed across plates. Require >=2 treatment records and >=2 controls on the relevant plate. There is a PC3/alpelisib 10uM P100 versus0.25uM RNA mismatch; do not match it. RNA reports 12328 Level3 genes but only978 are direct landmarks.

## Probe-isolated primary information regime
Leave one of the six source-study cellular backgrounds out at a time. Predict its L1000 response using source-study RNA responses at the same compound AND dose from >=2 other backgrounds. No target RNA treatment is an input or tuning label. Fixed probe set: staurosporine and vorinostat plus six SHA256-ordered eligible drugs, based solely on metadata; every probe must have >=2 P100 Level2 records at a common exact dose in all six backgrounds. Exclude all eight probe identities from all RNA query sets at every probe budget (0,2,4,8). Target P100 input is restricted to those probe treatments and controls; no query-drug P100 enters this regime. Count actual treatment/reference records, not only drug identities.

Compare zero, source-response mean (plus source-only fitted amplitude), RNA-baseline kernel, P100-control kernel, same-budget probe-magnitude kernel, full-probe kernel, full-probe+RNA-baseline kernel and a context-specific probe-identity permutation control. All kernels use the same source-only fitted missing-value transformation/scaling, free-intercept RBF ridge formula and small common hyperparameter grid. Select parameters/amplitude by nested leave-source-background-out validation, not held-target labels. Preserve full978-gene output. No choice of the best method after target scoring will be presented as prespecified superiority.

## Measurement and statistical limits
P100 primary inputs use Level2 log2 light/heavy ratios without plate-wide treatment-dependent offsets. Do not log these values again; missing values remain missing until source-only feature imputation. RNA outcome spaces are separately reported: log2 Level2 fluorescence relative to same-plate held reference, and published Level3 direct-landmark expression relative to the same reference. Neither is raw RNA-seq counts. Published Level3 has upstream cross-sample processing and is only a conditional processed-data benchmark. Split same-plate DMSO in deterministic well order into baseline A and scoring B; source fitting also uses B-defined responses, target B is withheld until predictions freeze. Splitting records does not establish independent culture/preparation. Feature selection, filtering and imputation must not use held-target outcome values. All candidates for both RNA spaces freeze before joint scoring. Report per-context MSE, centered/bias decomposition, amplitude, drug retrieval, information/cost ledger and failures; no biological p-value from treating genes or repeated rows as independent. Existing assay selection, few independent backgrounds and missing controls limit inference.
