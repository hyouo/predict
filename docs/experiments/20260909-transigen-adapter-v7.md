# Post-primary v7 extension: author-code TranSiGen adapter

Declared after v7 pharmacology-prior primary results were scored. This is retrospective adapter development on exposed L1000 data, not a blind test or a reproduction of the paper's strongest pretrained model.

## Native code and fixed training variant
Use unchanged TranSiGen class, forward pass and loss from myzhengSIMM/TranSiGen at e7582e7794f13d4dfddfb15e95e3ba7cd24bc07a, verified model.py Git blob d2de2b9e091cd84f4f32625dc07a1bba6e157ec3, with original MIT license retained. Random initialization and ECFP4 are options in the author's training script. No released weights or shRNA pretraining are loaded, because exposure cannot be controlled. Use n_genes=978, latent=100, encoder=[1200], decoder=[800], molecular embedding=[400], dropout=.1, beta=.1, Adam lr=.001, weight_decay=.00001, batch64 and seed364039. Training is CPU. Maximum300 epochs; validate every5 epochs, allow stopping after30 consecutive evaluated epochs without a new best (minimum50). Epoch is selected by equal-context effect-MSE on the six permitted validation contexts, not target test outcomes. Refit from the same initialization on 24 permitted contexts for the selected epoch count. Report whether the search hit its cap and the complete learning curve.

## Data and differences from native publication
Restrict adapter training to 1,038 compounds with unambiguous exact-CID canonical full SMILES in the fixed 20200324 sample table. No salt stripping, stereo merging, missing-structure zero imputation or untrusted pickle loading. Generate radius2/2048-bit Morgan fingerprints; not asserted byte-identical to the paper's archived ECFP. Actual paired source-level data consist of the mean observed treated measurement x2 and the exactly weighted per-plate B controls x1. Their difference matches the inherited effect within 6e-15. These are measured L1000 levels, not an artificial baseline+effect pseudo-level. 6,188 training and 2,284 validation source profiles are available. No target treatment or target B-reference is used. For held-context validation and test inference, supply only the allowed baseline A mean. The original network class/loss are unchanged; the data adapter and deterministic latent-mean inference wrapper are new and must be labeled. Deterministic mean inference is not asserted to equal the stochastic decoder expectation.

## Comparators and reporting
Score only common structure-available query rows, separately from the full v7 main table. Include zero effect, source mean, inherited RBF and target operator on those identical rows. Native molecule-only inference has less query-response information than the source-response methods; do not call such an asymmetric comparison fair superiority. Also fit on validation only a nonnegative convex fusion of the RBF source-response prediction and the native prediction, plus a calibrated native-only prediction. Keep missing-structure predictions missing rather than zero. Report all variants, total/centered/bias errors and drug retrieval, with controls' respective information inputs explicit. No claim to exceed State/MAP/PrePR-CT, no population-level inference, no automatic default-model update.

Model checkpoints use state_dict and weights_only loading; chunked foreground training stores RNG/optimizer state for exact continuation. Freeze checkpoint/selection/prediction hashes before any adapter target scoring. Preserve incomplete attempts and source-code provenance.
