## New hypothesis and matching
Source contexts and compounds have different repeatability. Use existing source treatment/control records, not additional target labels, to estimate a working variance of each averaged effect. Group all records sharing a physical plate before estimating variation. Shrink the variance toward a source-only global median, with three prior degrees of freedom; retain unavailable estimates as missing and impute only the variance (never a response) from the global median. Build a heterogeneous diagonal nugget in ordinary kriging, with an unpenalized constant, and compare to its constant-noise and shuffled-variance counterparts. All predictions use the complete source means (unchanged data budget). This variation includes batch/biological differences and is NOT identified pure technical noise.

Candidate modes: identity nugget; replicate-count-only; source context-average variance; drug-average variance; context-by-drug variance; within-drug shuffled context variances (seed9101). Variance ratios are clipped to [0.05,20] relative to the training-source median, and shrunk toward one on a common grid [0,0.5,1]. Kernel penalties [.01,.1,1,10,100]. The retained v7 response mean uses context-rank12 / drug-rank32 / prior penalties [.1,1,10], same-kernel residual innovation, blends [0,.5,1]. Scalar amplitude is fitted analytically on validation contexts, clipped to[0,1]. Separate selections for ordinary and tissue-excluded scenarios; retain every candidate, fixed-parameter attribution and adverse metrics. No target-guided gene exclusion or post-test hyperparameter change. Replicate proxies are computed from training sources for selection and train+validation sources for final refit. Retain total/centered/bias error, drug retrieval, amplitude and repeated reference-matched interaction risk. Primary comparison is selected heterogeneous versus selected identity-noise v7, not versus an obsolete weak baseline. Any later change is a dated exploratory extension.
