## Hypotheses and fixed prototypes
1. For known targets measured among landmark genes, mix an ordinary full-baseline RBF kernel with a target-expression kernel. 2. Expand target relevance through human Reactome pathways of 5-300 genes with at least 3 measured landmarks: average pathways equally per target, genes equally per pathway, then normalize the nonzero mask. This is an annotation-weighted kernel, not a causal model. Both use kernel mixture eta in {0,.25,.5,1}, ridge penalty {.01,.1,1,10,100}, free support-centred intercept and the same analytic [0,1] validation amplitude as the full-RBF comparator. Unknown priors fall back exactly to full RBF at matched parameters. Full-baseline linear kernel and prior-best RBF are mandatory controls.
3. Fit a low-dimensional baseline-by-target-fingerprint residual correction on leave-source-context-out residuals of full RBF. Baseline rank <=6, target-fingerprint rank <=16, ridge {.1,1,10,100}, residual strength {0,.25,.5,1}, and matched analytic amplitude. All preprocessing uses permitted sources, not held target effects. This family and its no-correction/randomized-prior controls are named separately; extra search capacity is disclosed.
